CVR Campus Visitor Clearance & Entry Portal

Welcome to the official visitor management and gate access portal for CVR College of Engineering (Promoted by Cherabuddi Education Society, Estd. 2001). To ensure campus safety and efficient academic scheduling across our 33-acre Vastunagar campus, all external visitors, academic examiners, campus placement partners, vendor personnel, and parents are required to pre-register and secure department clearance prior to physical arrival.

Register a Campus Visit Check Visit Clearance Status
Mandatory Department Clearance Prior to Gate Entry
Visitors must obtain an official approval status and Reference Number (CVR-2026-XXXX) before reporting to the security gates. CVR Campus Security personnel at Gate 1 will not issue visitor entry badges to unapproved individuals without prior administrative clearance.

Standard Campus Visitor Procedure

STEP 01

Submit Online Registration

Fill in your contact details, host engineering department (CSE, ECE, EEE, IT, Mechanical, Civil, Placement Cell, or Admin), faculty member to meet, and expected arrival schedule.

Estimated form time: ~2 minutes
STEP 02

Department Clearance Review

The Head of Department or Administrative Officer verifies your appointment and approves the visit. You can check the approval status anytime using your Reference Number and Email.

Processed within 1 business day
STEP 03

Check-in at Gate 1 (Vastunagar)

Upon arrival at CVR Main Entrance (Gate 1), present your government photo ID (Aadhaar, Driving License, or Passport) and Reference Number to receive an official visitor pass and vehicle tag.

Physical ID verification mandatory

CVR Campus Access Guidelines & Code of Conduct

  • Mandatory Photo ID: All visitors must carry and present a valid government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar card, Voter ID, Driving License, or Passport) at the security gatehouse.
  • Visitor Badge Display: The issued visitor badge must be visibly worn above the waist at all times on campus grounds and in departmental buildings.
  • Designated Campus Parking: Visitor four-wheelers and two-wheelers must be parked only in designated Visitor Parking (near Main Gate / Central Library). Driving beyond student pedestrian zones is strictly prohibited.
  • Lab & High-Security Areas: Entry to Advanced Computing Labs, High Voltage Labs, and Server Rooms requires continuous faculty host escort.
  • Departure Check-Out: Before leaving the campus, surrender your visitor pass at Gate 1 security desk to log departure.
